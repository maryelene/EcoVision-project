Segregation Reference

♻️ Know Your Waste

Learn the difference between biodegradable and non-biodegradable materials.

🌿 Biodegradable Waste

These are materials that can naturally decompose through microorganisms under suitable environmental conditions.

Common Examples
  • 🍌 Fruit peels
  • 🍂 Dry leaves
  • 🥬 Vegetable scraps
  • 🍚 Food leftovers
  • 📄 Untreated paper

🧴 Non-Biodegradable Waste

These are materials that do not easily decompose naturally and may remain in the environment for a long time.

Common Examples
  • 🧴 Plastic bottles
  • 🛍️ Plastic wrappers
  • 🥫 Metal cans
  • 🍾 Glass bottles
  • 🧵 Synthetic materials
